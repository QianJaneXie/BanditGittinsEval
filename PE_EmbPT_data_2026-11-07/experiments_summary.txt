Experiment summary | MMLU PE-EmbPT
=================================

Question
--------
How does the 39-feature arm-only logistic baseline change when it uses
PromptEval-style question effects and full-row estimates? A second
comparison holds that estimator fixed and changes question sampling.

Methods
-------
Each subject has 1,500 candidates formed by crossing 15 LLMs with 100
prompt templates. All three BAI variants use the same 39 arm covariates.
These are 25 principal components of pretrained DPR prompt embeddings
and 14 LLM indicators, with one LLM as the reference.

  logistic_39       Arm-only L2 logistic regression. Rank by fitted
                    arm score. Sample an unseen question uniformly.
  rasch39_random    Add one shared identity coefficient per question.
                    Score arms with observed responses and fitted
                    probabilities for unseen responses. Use the
                    same random-question sampling rule as logistic_39.
  rasch39_balanced  Use the same Rasch estimator and recommendation.
                    Sample among the arm's least-covered unseen
                    questions using column counts over all arms.

The Rasch fit uses observed binary outcomes, native L2 LIBLINEAR
logistic regression with C=100, and no separate intercept. It fits 39+N
coefficients for a subject with N questions. The Rasch-versus-logistic
comparison changes both the fitted model and the scoring rule. The
balanced-versus-random Rasch comparison holds both fixed.

The joint replay uses five successive-halving phases at 2%, 4%, 6%,
8%, and 10% of response cells. Active counts before elimination are
1,500, 750, 375, 187, and 93. The upstream phase formula would give
11 phases on a 1,500-arm pool. The original PromptEval experiment
instead ranked prompts within each model and selected its final
recommendation using the observed empirical mean among surviving arms.
Here each phase reports the top-scoring active arm.

Results
-------
The 10% unit-cost endpoint gives equal weight to all 57 MMLU subjects.

  Method                 Mean simple regret
  logistic_39                  0.02171928
  rasch39_random               0.00951678
  rasch39_balanced             0.00905031

Balanced Rasch improved on logistic_39 in all 57 subjects at this
endpoint. The mean paired difference was -0.01266897 with a subject
standard error of 0.00100077. The 10% price-weighted checkpoint means
were 0.02177324, 0.00978393, and 0.00943791 in the same order. That
view reprices the same unit-budget BAI histories.

On the 19 Figure 3 subjects, both Rasch variants had higher cohort
mean regret than GittinsEval-S at all 36 comparable checkpoints.
Each Rasch variant had lower mean regret than GittinsEval-G at four
of those checkpoints and higher regret at 32. These counts describe
the reported checkpoint means. Per-seed Gittins tests and claims about
the intervening parts of the curves are outside the scope of the table.

Validation
----------
The Rasch archive audit checked 57 subjects, 2,280 trajectories, and
11,400 phase records. The report audit rebuilt 14,088 plotted points
across 40 curves and all four checkpoint tables. On the college
chemistry pilot, flipping the unseen responses changed the evaluation
oracle while preserving every first-phase Rasch arm score and
recommendation under both samplers.

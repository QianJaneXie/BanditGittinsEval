Experiment summary | MMLU PE-EmbPT interaction design
====================================================

The interaction design had higher mean regret at the 2% through 8% unit
checkpoints under both samplers. At the 10% unit-cost endpoint, the
random-question variants were close on average, while the balanced
interaction variant had higher regret than balanced concatenation.
Both interaction variants improved on the arm-only logistic baseline
in all 57 subjects at that endpoint.

Design
------
All 57 MMLU subjects were replayed with 20 seeds for each of two new
variants, for 2,280 trajectories and 11,400 recorded phases. The comparison
reuses the first version's two Rasch variants and its 39-feature logistic
baseline. Both PE designs use the same saved pretrained prompt coordinates,
14 model offsets, and N question effects. Concatenation shares 25 prompt
coefficients across models. The interaction design assigns 25 coefficients
to each of 15 models, giving 389 arm coefficients.

Within each sampler, both designs use the same five-phase, 10%-of-cells
protocol, observed-plus-imputed arm score, and L2 LIBLINEAR at C=100. The
parameterizations give different penalties to equivalent prompt scores.
The experiment compares the two fitted designs under their common
coefficient penalty and original feature scales.

At 10% unit cost, the paired interaction-minus-concatenation difference
was -0.00003293 with subject SE 0.00046207 for random questions and
+0.00112382 with subject SE 0.00040516 for balanced questions. Across
subjects, the random interaction variant had lower, tied, and higher
regret in 26, 2, and 29 subjects. The corresponding balanced counts were
19, 1, and 37.

At the 10% price-weighted checkpoint, concatenated and interaction means
were 0.00978393 and 0.00990546 with random questions, and 0.00943791 and
0.01044232 with balanced questions. The logistic mean was 0.02177324.
The price-weighted view reprices the same observation-budgeted histories.

-------------------
The 19 Figure 3 subjects form four cohorts. There are 36 comparable
cohort checkpoints across the unit and price-weighted views. The four
2% price-weighted cohort rows have incomplete PE coverage.

The interaction variant had lower mean regret than the matched
concatenated variant at 9 of 36 random-question checkpoints and 8 of 36
balanced-question checkpoints. The random interaction variant's single
checkpoint below GittinsEval-G was Hard-Small at 8% price-weighted spend.
These counts describe cohort means at the listed checkpoints. Per-seed
Gittins tests and performance at intervening budgets are outside this
comparison. The 9.9% unit-cost checkpoint precedes the final BAI phase
at 10%, so use the all-subject table for the separate endpoint.

Validation
----------
The new archives cover all 57 subjects, 2,280 runs, and 11,400 phases.
There were zero recorded solver convergence warnings. The report provides
19,212 plotted points and both aggregate and subject checkpoint tables.
See validation.json for the independent archive, portable export, and
figure reproduction checks.
